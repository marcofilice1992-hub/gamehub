# 1. Parcel Panic

- Slot: 1
- Id: `parcel-panic`
- Group: Chaos kitchen
- Pace: Frantic
- Size: M
- Source: the "15 couch co-op games to build" doc, 13 Nov 2026

A sorting depot where the parcels never stop arriving. Shifts last 3 minutes.

- **Loop:** Parcels roll in on a conveyor. Each label shows up to three steps (weigh, wrap, stamp). Finished parcels go on the truck of the matching colour before it leaves.
- **Why it needs two:** Stations sit on opposite sides of a belt you cannot cross, so parcels are thrown across. Fragile ones need both of you to carry.
- **Controls:** Stick to move, A pick up or put down, X use station, B throw.
- **First playable:** One depot, 3 stations, 2 truck colours, a star score.
- **Later:** 12 depots with moving belts, a forklift, night shifts with the lights out.
- **Hard part:** Reading parcel labels at a glance from the couch. Icons, not text.
