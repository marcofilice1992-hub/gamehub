# 6. Polarity

- Slot: 6
- Id: `polarity`
- Group: Tethered and puzzle
- Pace: Medium
- Size: M
- Source: the "15 couch co-op games to build" doc, 13 Nov 2026

Each of you is a magnet, one red and one blue. Opposite poles attract and like poles repel.

- **Loop:** Cross short platform rooms using each other and the metal in the level.
- **Why it needs two:** Flip your pole to fling your partner across a gap, or lock together to cross a magnetic ceiling.
- **Controls:** Stick to move, A jump, X flip polarity, hold the right trigger to strengthen your field.
- **First playable:** 10 rooms.
- **Later:** 36 rooms, moving magnets, a speedrun timer.
- **Hard part:** Tuning the force so it is predictable. The falloff curve needs a lot of playtesting.
