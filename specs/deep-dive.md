# 12. Deep Dive

- Slot: 12
- Id: `deep-dive`
- Group: Run-based action
- Pace: Medium
- Size: M
- Source: the "15 couch co-op games to build" doc, 13 Nov 2026

Dig down from a surface pod on one shared air hose. Dives last 6 to 10 minutes.

- **Loop:** Dig, carry ore back up, spend it on upgrades, go deeper. A dive ends when the air runs out.
- **Why it needs two:** The single hose keeps you close. One digs while the other hauls. Heavy relics need both to carry, and someone has to work the winch.
- **Controls:** Stick to dig in a direction, A carry, X place a ladder or light.
- **First playable:** A generated shaft, 3 ores, 5 upgrades, an air limit.
- **Later:** Creatures, cave-ins, 3 biomes, a bottom to reach.
- **Hard part:** Level generation that always leaves a route down.
