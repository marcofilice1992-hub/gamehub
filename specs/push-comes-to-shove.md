# 5. Push Comes to Shove

- Slot: 5
- Id: `push-comes-to-shove`
- Group: Tethered and puzzle
- Pace: Calm
- Size: S
- Source: the "15 couch co-op games to build" doc, 13 Nov 2026

Grid puzzle rooms with no timer and unlimited undo.

- **Loop:** Push crates onto targets to open the exit. Each room takes 2 to 10 minutes of talking it through.
- **Why it needs two:** Heavy crates move only when both push. Some pressure plates must be held by one player while the other passes.
- **Controls:** D-pad or stick to step, B undo, Y restart the room.
- **First playable:** 15 rooms with 3 mechanics: heavy crate, pressure plate, one-way gate.
- **Later:** 60 rooms, ice floors, conveyors, a level editor.
- **Hard part:** Level design, not code. The engine is a day's work. A solver that proves each room is solvable is worth building alongside it.
