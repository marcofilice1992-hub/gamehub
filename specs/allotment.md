# 13. Allotment

- Slot: 13
- Id: `allotment`
- Group: Cosy and long-form
- Pace: Calm
- Size: M
- Source: the "15 couch co-op games to build" doc, 13 Nov 2026

A shared garden plot with no fail state. A season is 10 minutes and the garden persists between sessions.

- **Loop:** Plant, water, harvest, and fill the neighbours' order boxes to unlock seeds and tools.
- **Why it needs two:** Big jobs go faster together: a two-person wheelbarrow, one pumping while the other waters. Nothing breaks if you split up.
- **Controls:** Stick to move, A use tool, X swap tool, Y carry.
- **First playable:** 12 tiles, 5 crops, watering, 1 neighbour, a save file.
- **Later:** 4 seasons with weather, a greenhouse, bees, decorating the plot.
- **Hard part:** Pacing the unlocks so there is always something new within a session or two.
