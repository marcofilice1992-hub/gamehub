# 10. Tank for Two

- Slot: 10
- Id: `tank-for-two`
- Group: Run-based action
- Pace: Frantic
- Size: S
- Source: the "15 couch co-op games to build" doc, 13 Nov 2026

One tank, two seats. A run is 10 waves, about 12 minutes.

- **Loop:** Survive arena waves. Between waves, pick one of three upgrades.
- **Why it needs two:** It is one vehicle. The Driver positions, dodges and collects ammo. The Gunner aims, picks the shell type and reloads.
- **Controls:** Driver: left stick to steer, right trigger to boost. Gunner: stick to aim, right trigger to fire, X to reload on a timing bar, bumpers to change shell.
- **First playable:** One arena, 4 enemy types, 10 waves, 12 upgrades.
- **Later:** A boss every 5 waves, 3 arenas, seat swaps between waves.
- **Hard part:** Enemy variety. The engine itself is simple.
