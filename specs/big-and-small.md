# 9. Big & Small

- Slot: 9
- Id: `big-and-small`
- Group: Asymmetric
- Pace: Medium
- Size: M
- Source: the "15 couch co-op games to build" doc, 13 Nov 2026

A puzzle-platformer for a strong slow character and a quick light one.

- **Loop:** Get both characters through each room. Rooms take 3 to 6 minutes.
- **Why it needs two:** Big moves blocks, breaks cracked walls and throws Small. Small fits through vents, rides fans and reaches high switches.
- **Controls:** Stick to move, A jump, X act. Big also has Y to pick up and throw Small.
- **First playable:** 10 rooms.
- **Later:** 40 rooms and a hub. Swap characters at any checkpoint.
- **Hard part:** Throw arcs and room design. Small asks for more platforming and Big for more planning, so pick seats to suit.
