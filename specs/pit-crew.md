# 2. Pit Crew

- Slot: 2
- Id: `pit-crew`
- Group: Chaos kitchen
- Pace: Frantic
- Size: S
- Source: the "15 couch co-op games to build" doc, 13 Nov 2026

Service race cars before the queue backs up. Races last 4 minutes.

- **Loop:** A car pulls in every 20 to 30 seconds with a fault list: tyres, fuel, bodywork, engine. Each fix is a short controller input. Three cars waiting ends the race.
- **Why it needs two:** One holds the jack while the other changes tyres. One holds the fuel hose while the other opens the valve.
- **Controls:** Stick to move, A grab. Then per job: rotate the stick to unbolt, hold the trigger to fuel to a line, tap X to hammer panels.
- **First playable:** One bay, 4 job types, endless mode with rising speed.
- **Later:** An 8-track championship, weather that changes the tyre type, a second bay.
- **Hard part:** Making four inputs feel distinct. Rumble does a lot of the work.
