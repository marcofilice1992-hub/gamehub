# 11. Swarm Night

- Slot: 11
- Id: `swarm-night`
- Group: Run-based action
- Pace: Frantic
- Size: S
- Source: the "15 couch co-op games to build" doc, 13 Nov 2026

A top-down survival run where weapons fire on their own. A run lasts 12 minutes.

- **Loop:** Enemies pour in. Collect XP, level up, choose upgrades, beat the boss at the end.
- **Why it needs two:** XP is shared and you take turns choosing upgrades. Pair upgrades need both of you: one player's frost slows enemies, the other's shots shatter them. Stand beside a downed partner for 3 seconds to revive them.
- **Controls:** Left stick only, plus A to pick upgrades. The easiest game here to pick up.
- **First playable:** 1 map, 6 weapons, 8 enemy types, a final boss.
- **Later:** 4 characters, 15 pair upgrades, unlocks that persist between runs.
- **Hard part:** Keeping 500 or more enemies smooth on Canvas, which needs a spatial grid for collisions. Then balance.
