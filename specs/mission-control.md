# 7. Mission Control

- Slot: 7
- Id: `mission-control`
- Group: Asymmetric
- Pace: Medium
- Size: M
- Source: the "15 couch co-op games to build" doc, 13 Nov 2026

One of you flies a lander, the other runs its power console. Missions take 3 to 5 minutes.

- **Loop:** Land on a series of moons. The Pilot has thrust and rotation. The Engineer routes limited power between engine, shields, scanner and landing gear, and repairs faults.
- **Why it needs two:** The gear will not deploy without power. The scanner only shows terrain while powered. Shields drain the engine. You talk constantly.
- **Controls:** Pilot: stick to rotate, right trigger to thrust. Engineer: stick to select a system, A and B to add or remove power, a timing press to repair.
- **First playable:** 5 moons, 4 systems, fuel and hull limits.
- **Later:** 20 missions, cargo runs, storms, swapping seats each mission.
- **Hard part:** Balancing the load so neither seat is ever idle.
