# 8. Heist Night

- Slot: 8
- Id: `heist-night`
- Group: Asymmetric
- Pace: Medium
- Size: L
- Source: the "15 couch co-op games to build" doc, 13 Nov 2026

One sneaks through the building, the other hacks it. Heists take 8 to 12 minutes.

- **Loop:** The Infiltrator gets to the vault and back out. The Hacker moves a cursor over the same top-down view to loop cameras, open doors, cut lights and call lifts, each on a cooldown.
- **Why it needs two:** There are more guards and cameras than one person can dodge, and doors only open from the Hacker's side.
- **Controls:** Infiltrator: stick to move, A interact, B crouch. Hacker: stick to move the cursor, A trigger a device, bumpers to change floor.
- **First playable:** 3 buildings with fixed guard patrols, vision cones, cameras and doors.
- **Later:** 12 heists, alarms that escalate instead of ending the run, loot that buys gadgets.
- **Hard part:** Guard behaviour, vision cones and hand-built levels. This is the largest build of the 15.
