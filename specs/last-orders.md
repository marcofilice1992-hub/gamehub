# 3. Last Orders

- Slot: 3
- Id: `last-orders`
- Group: Chaos kitchen
- Pace: Frantic
- Size: M
- Source: the "15 couch co-op games to build" doc, 13 Nov 2026

Run a cocktail bar through a 4-minute service.

- **Loop:** Tickets appear along the bar. Build each drink: glass, ice, pour to the marked level, shake or stir, garnish, serve. Tips shrink the longer a customer waits.
- **Why it needs two:** The bar splits into a pouring end and a prep end with a pass shelf between. Rounds of four drinks need both ends working at once.
- **Controls:** Stick to move, A pick up. The right trigger pours and is analog, so squeezing harder pours faster. Waggle the right stick to shake.
- **First playable:** 6 recipes, one bar layout.
- **Later:** A recipe book that grows over 10 nights, regulars with fixed orders, happy-hour rushes.
- **Hard part:** The recipe and ticket data model, and showing an order clearly in a small space.
