# 4. Tether

- Slot: 4
- Id: `tether`
- Group: Tethered and puzzle
- Pace: Medium
- Size: M
- Source: the "15 couch co-op games to build" doc, 13 Nov 2026

A side-on platformer with a 6-metre elastic rope joining you. Levels take 2 to 3 minutes.

- **Loop:** Reach the exit together. The rope stretches, swings and snags on pegs.
- **Why it needs two:** One plants their feet to become an anchor while the other swings or climbs. The rope also pulls switches and carries items.
- **Controls:** Stick to move, A jump, left trigger anchor, right trigger reel in.
- **First playable:** 10 levels teaching swing, anchor and climb.
- **Later:** 40 levels across 4 worlds, hazards that cut the rope, time medals.
- **Hard part:** Rope physics that feels good. Budget the first weekend for feel alone.
