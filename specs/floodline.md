# 15. Floodline

- Slot: 15
- Id: `floodline`
- Group: Cosy and long-form
- Pace: Calm
- Size: M
- Source: the "15 couch co-op games to build" doc, 13 Nov 2026

A turn-based board game against a rising river. A game takes 20 to 30 minutes.

- **Loop:** A river town on a hex map floods a little more each round. Each player takes 3 actions per turn: move, sandbag, pump or evacuate. Save 8 of 12 districts by round 15.
- **Why it needs two:** Each role has one power, such as the Engineer pumping two hexes at once, and the flood outpaces either role alone. You plan each turn aloud.
- **Controls:** Stick to select a hex, A confirm, B undo.
- **First playable:** One map, 4 roles, a flood deck, win and lose conditions.
- **Later:** 5 maps, difficulty levels, event cards, a linked campaign.
- **Hard part:** Balance. A script that plays thousands of random games and reports the win rate settles it faster than playtesting.
