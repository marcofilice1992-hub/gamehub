# 14. Slow Boat

- Slot: 14
- Id: `slow-boat`
- Group: Cosy and long-form
- Pace: Calm
- Size: L
- Source: the "15 couch co-op games to build" doc, 13 Nov 2026

Sail between islands delivering goods. Sessions are open-ended; a crossing takes 2 to 4 minutes.

- **Loop:** Buy goods cheaply on one island, sell them on another, upgrade the boat, sail further.
- **Why it needs two:** One steers. The other trims the sail to the wind, fishes off the back and reads the map. The boat is fastest when both do their job, and docking needs one on the rope.
- **Controls:** Helm: stick to steer. Crew: stick for sail angle, A to cast and reel, Y for the map. Both press B to swap seats.
- **First playable:** 5 islands, wind, 4 goods, one upgrade path.
- **Later:** 20 islands revealed by exploring, storms, passengers with stories, a lighthouse to restore.
- **Hard part:** World content. Sailing is quick to build. Islands, goods and stories are a long tail.
